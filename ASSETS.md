## Design research

- Nothing’s Glyph Matrix informed the use of a monochrome dot grid and its legibility at small sizes. [Nothing Phone (3)](https://nothing.tech/products/phone-3)
- Apple’s material design informed the translucent navigation and the shift in separation when content scrolls under it. [Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)
- SCHOTT’s optical materials informed the aperture’s polished edges and refractive highlights. [SCHOTT Optical Glass](https://www.schott.com/en-gb/products/optical-glass-p1000267)
- Teenage Engineering’s TP–7 informed the mix of compact functional labels and product-led art direction. [TP–7 Field Recorder](https://teenage.engineering/products/tp-7)

These references served as research. All runtime imagery and fonts are served locally.

The Windows icon is a PNG rasterization of the original Catch monogram, with an ICO wrapper for the portable executable. Release screenshots show the running application with authored example captures. Benchmark images and PDFs are original synthetic fixtures generated by the fixture script.

The 0.2.2 showcase ships original PDFs, rasterized PDF previews and recreated demonstration screenshots. The catalog, visual assets and generator are part of Catch. Third-party research and documentation appear as publisher links. [Showcase inventory and provenance](SHOWCASE.md)

## Aperture asset prompt

> Product-mockup asset for Catch, a premium personal clipboard app. One original sculptural optical-glass object, a thick squared torus with rounded square corners and a real circular aperture, like a precision cast-glass aperture ring. Oblique three-quarter angle, rotated about 25 degrees in frame, showing front bevel and volumetric thickness and transparent transmission. Crystal-clear polished borosilicate glass with a vivid safety-orange inner edge and an orange caustic focused through the lower interior face, tiny airy bubbles optional, strong white studio strip reflections, real photographic material physics, the dark edge reflections of finely machined glass. Art-directed contemporary industrial technology product photography. Isolated centered cutout, readable silhouette, generous margin around entire object. Transparent background and no floor or detached shadow. No text, no branding, no chrome body, no additional objects, no neon halos, no cartoon render, no opaque yellow plastic.
